01 — The Premise

Exercise first.
Diet second.

Most plans start in the kitchen and fail there. This one starts on the road. A daily 30–45 minute jog plus 3–4 bodyweight strength sessions builds a calorie buffer big enough that food stops being a daily negotiation.

The diet isn't a diet. It's three rules that target the places calories actually hide: a weak breakfast, deep-fried food, and late-night snacking. Everything else — eat freely, around 2,400 cal.

Built for a 40-year-old with a desk job in Noida. No gym membership required. Just shoes, a park, an air fryer, and a clock.

Burn more than you eat — without counting what you eat.

02 — The Diet

Three Rules.
Nothing Else.

Each rule shuts down one specific calorie leak. Follow all three every day and eat whatever else you want — the jogs handle the rest.

01
Rule One · Breakfast

3 Eggs.
Every Morning.

Start every day with three eggs. The protein and fat keep you full for hours, so you naturally eat less through the rest of the day — without trying.
